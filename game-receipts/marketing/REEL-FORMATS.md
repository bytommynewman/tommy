# Two formats lifted from reference reels (Oct 21 2026)

Watched: owen.income "Watch me pay my rent off in less than 30 seconds" (21.6K likes, 727 comments) and jack.tries1 "Easiest guide fr — comment UGC".

What makes them work: (1) a stakes hook + a stopwatch, (2) numbered steps so it reads as a tutorial, not an ad, (3) real screen footage, (4) a **comment-keyword CTA** ("comment X for the guide") — comments juice the algorithm and the creator DMs the link.

## Format A · "Speedrun split-screen" (owen.income style)

Layout 1080×1920:
- **Top 0–760:** creator half — the UGC talking-head clips (Kyle / MVP pick) cropped to the face, or the person reacting. Stopwatch overlay (white mono digits on dark pill, top-right of this panel) counting from 00:00.
- **Middle band 760–860:** black bar, white bold step caption: "Step 1: …".
- **Bottom 860–1920:** real app screen recording (phone capture, scaled to width).
- **Hook (0–2 s, over the top panel):** white bold text on black box.

Spots:
- **A1 "MVP pick in under 10 seconds"** — Hook: "Watch me lock my MVP pick in under 10 seconds". Steps: 1 Pick your league → 2 Search your guy → 3 Pick a take (Wins an award · MVP) → 4 Place it. Stamped. Graded when it's done. Stopwatch stops ~00:09. End beat: leaderboard.
- **A2 "Prove you called it"** — Hook: "How to prove you called it (takes 8 seconds)". Steps: 1 Pick your team → 2 Pick how far they go → 3 Place take → 4 Screenshot the receipt when it hits. End beat: resolved "Cashed" receipt + Prestige.
- CTA end card: "Comment **RECEIPT** and I'll send you the link" + "Free · No betting".

## Format B · "Filmed my screen" POV (jack.tries1 style)

Looks like a phone filming another screen: app footage on a slightly tilted/perspective screen plane, gentle handheld drift, mild screen glare/grain, and ONE short lowercase caption at a time, centered lower-third, white with black outline ("pick your player", "pick your take", "hit place", "it stamps the date", "graded when it's done", "now you're on the leaderboard").
- **B1 "easiest way to win every argument"** — hook caption "easiest way to win every sports argument fr 😭", then the steps above over the take-builder flow → feed stamp → leaderboard.
- Audio: none baked in — post with a trending sound from the in-app library.
- CTA in post caption: "comment RECEIPT for the link 🧾".

**Best version of B is real:** Tommy films his own phone/laptop showing the app (shot list below) — real hands beat any simulation. The rendered version is the stand-in.
