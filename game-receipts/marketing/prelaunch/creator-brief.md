## The app in one line
Game Receipts lets you lock a sports take on any player or team, get a date-stamped receipt, and get graded when it happens. **Free. No betting. No money.** Just bragging rights. Tagline: **Call it. Lock it. Prove it.** Link: the waitlist page in our bio / the link we give you.

## Payout
- **$1.00 to $2.00 per 1,000 qualified views** (start at $1.00 CPM; top-performing creators move to $2.00 after their first approved batch).
- **Campaign budget cap: $300 to $500 total.** Payouts stop when the pool is spent; earlier approved views are paid first. Per-creator cap: **$75** per payout cycle so one viral clip can't drain the pool.
- A view counts if it is on TikTok, Instagram Reels, or YouTube Shorts, public, on your own account, within the first 14 days after posting, and not botted or incentivised. Minimum 1,000 views before any payout. Payouts are reviewed manually and paid on Whop (or the platform we run it on) every 7 days.
- Original, repeat-the-same-clip spam or reposts of our videos with no changes will not count.

## What to make
Short vertical videos, **15 to 45 seconds**, 1080×1920, a hook in the first 1.5 seconds, a caption on every beat (assume sound off), one clear call to action ("link in bio" or "comment RECEIPT").

Formats that work:
1. **Group-chat story:** your friend "called it". Show the text thread, then say "now we get receipts."
2. **Speedrun:** stopwatch on screen, "I locked my MVP pick in under 10 seconds."
3. **Screen walkthrough:** film your phone or laptop (or use the screen clips we supply) showing pick a league → pick a take → place it → LOCKED stamp.
4. **Hot-take reaction:** read an old bad take, say "this needs a receipt."
5. **Challenge:** "Lock a take with me" / duet and stitch encouraged.

You get a **clip pack** (screen recordings, `assets/` teasers, the logo). Use our footage or film your own app screen. Never use ESPN/league clips or highlights.

## Don't
- **No betting framing.** No odds, picks-for-money, parlays, units, sportsbook names, "lock in your bet," "win money," or "free bets." This is not a gambling app and can never be pitched as one.
- **No team, league, or sponsor logos**, no ESPN or broadcast footage, no jerseys with marks, no real athlete headshots as the main visual. Naming a team or player in text or speech is fine.
- No fake earnings, fake testimonials, or "I made $X" claims. Don't say Game Receipts is live if it isn't.
- Don't target or feature anyone under 13; no content aimed at minors for gambling-adjacent topics. Keep it clean: no harassment, slurs, or attacks on real people or players.
- Don't edit our end card or tagline, and don't buy views or use bots. Violations void payouts and remove you from the program.

## AI label rules
- If any person, voice, or scene in your video is **AI-generated** (AI avatar, AI voice clone, AI b-roll that looks real), turn on the platform's AI label: TikTok "AI-generated content", Instagram "AI info", YouTube "altered or synthetic content". Also say so in the caption if it isn't obvious.
- Never present an AI person as a real customer or real fan. Real faces and your own voice are preferred and perform better.
- Lightly edited or filtered real footage doesn't need the label; synthetic people do.
