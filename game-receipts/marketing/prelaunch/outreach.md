# Outreach — micro-creators and UGC creators

Goal: 20 sports TikTok/IG creators at 5K to 100K followers (free early access + their own leaderboard; paid tier $50 to $150 per video) and a bench of ~10 real-human UGC videos at roughly $40 to $100 each.

Rules: find people manually from your own feed or search (hashtags like #sportstok, #nbatok, #fantasyfootball); keep the list in `outreach.csv`; do not scrape, buy lists, or auto-DM. Send DMs yourself from the brand account, one at a time, personalise the first line, one follow-up after 4 days, then stop. Never promise money outside the rates you have decided. Disclosure (#ad/#partner) and no betting framing apply to everyone (see `creator-brief.md`). Nothing here has been sent.

Fill-in tokens: `{{first_name}}`, `{{specific_video}}`, `{{waitlist_url}}`, `{{your_name}}`.

## Template 1 — Early access, free seed (first touch)
> Hey {{first_name}}, loved your {{specific_video}}. I'm building Game Receipts: you lock a sports take (player/team, from a dropdown), it gets a date-stamped receipt, and it's graded when it happens. Free, no betting. You'd get early access before launch and your own leaderboard of followers' takes. No obligation to post anything. Want me to send the link?

## Template 2 — Paid video offer (they've replied or have a media kit)
> Thanks {{first_name}}! We're paying $50 to $150 per video depending on reach for one 20-40s video showing Game Receipts (I send a brief, a clip pack and approved hooks; you keep your voice). Needs #ad/#partner and "Free · No betting" in the post, no team logos, no betting talk. We'd also give you early access and a Founding Member badge. What's your rate for a single TikTok/Reel? I can send the brief now.

## Template 3 — Group-chat captain / audience play (for creators with a community)
> Hey {{first_name}}, your {{specific_video}} comments are basically everyone arguing who called it. Game Receipts fixes that: every take gets a date-stamped receipt and gets graded. Free, no betting. I can set your community a leaderboard on launch day and give you a referral link: 5 friends joined = Founding Member badge and early access, no payment needed. Interested in being one of the first creators on it?

## Template 4 — UGC marketplace creator (Billo / Insense / Fiverr / Collabstr brief message)
> Hi {{first_name}}! I'm looking for a real person (not AI) to film a 20 to 30 second vertical UGC video for Game Receipts, a free sports-takes app (lock a take, get a date-stamped receipt, get graded; no betting). Deliverable: 1 video, 1080×1920, talking to camera, hook in the first 2 seconds, plus a 3 to 5 second reaction or point-at-screen shot. I provide the script options, hooks and screen recordings; you film yourself only. Budget: $40 to $100 depending on usage rights (paid ads: 30 days). No team logos or jerseys, no betting words. Raw file + captioned cut if you can. Timeline: 5 days. Can you take it?

## After they say yes
Send `creator-brief.md`, the clip pack (`assets/` + `out/spot-*.mp4` screen footage), and the posting checklist. Log everything in `outreach.csv` (statuses: `new`, `contacted`, `replied`, `agreed`, `posted`, `paid`, `declined`, `ghosted`).

## Tracking sheet
`outreach.csv` columns: `handle, platform, followers, niche, contacted_on, status, rate, notes` (header only, no rows).
