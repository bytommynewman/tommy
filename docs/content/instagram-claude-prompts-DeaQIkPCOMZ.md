## Slide 2: Find what actually goes viral

Stop guessing what to post. Find the patterns behind content people already watch and share.

> Analyze these 5 high-performing posts in the [niche] niche: [attach examples]. Break down their hooks, visual styles, pacing, emotional triggers, and engagement. Identify the patterns they share, then generate 10 original content ideas for my faceless Instagram page using those patterns. Include a hook and visual concept for each.

## Slide 3: Write scripts people finish

Turn each idea into a short video that keeps people watching.

> Write a short-form Instagram Reel script about [topic] for [target audience]. Open with a strong hook in the first 2 seconds. Build curiosity, remove unnecessary setup, and deliver a clear payoff. Keep it under 30 seconds. Include voiceover, on-screen text, and visual directions for each scene. Finish with a natural CTA to [desired action].

## Slide 4: Create stronger hooks

Give people a reason to stop scrolling immediately.

> Create 10 hooks for this Instagram Reel: [paste script]. Use different angles, including curiosity, surprise, a common mistake, a specific benefit, and a bold opinion. Keep each hook under 12 words and make it specific to [target audience]. Avoid generic clickbait and unsupported claims. Rank the strongest 3 and suggest an opening visual for each.

## Slide 5: Turn attention into sales

Connect your content to an offer your audience actually wants.

> Create 5 Instagram Reel concepts that connect [audience problem] to my [product or offer]. Each Reel should teach something useful, demonstrate a relevant result or use case, and naturally lead to the offer. Include a hook, a short script, a visual proof idea, and a comment-keyword CTA. Use only the results and evidence I provide: [insert proof].

## Slide 6: Build a repeatable content system

Turn your best ideas into a clear daily workflow.

> Build a 7-day content workflow for my faceless Instagram page in [niche]. Use these content examples and performance data: [insert]. For each day, include a topic, hook, short script, visual concept, caption, and CTA. Organize production into batch tasks. Explain which steps can be automated, which need my review, and how to use performance results to improve the next batch.

## Slide 7: Create your own AI avatar

Give your page a recognizable character without filming yourself.

> Create a detailed image-generation prompt for a realistic adult AI avatar for my [niche] Instagram page. Define a distinctive face, hairstyle, wardrobe, and signature setting that fit [target audience]. Include natural skin texture, subtle imperfections, realistic lighting, and photographic detail. Then write matching prompts for a front portrait, two three-quarter views, and a full-body shot, preserving the same identity across every image.
