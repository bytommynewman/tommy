# Channel brand kit — Money Under the Hood

Formerly "The Ledger" (the folder keeps that name). Picked for clicks: it says
money, promises a mechanism, and works as a handle.

## Name options considered

| Name | Why / why not |
|---|---|
| **Money Under the Hood** (picked) | Says the topic and the angle in four words; handle `@moneyunderthehood` reads cleanly; covers tech too |
| The 30-Second Ledger | Honest about format, but "ledger" is a cold word on a thumbnail |
| Plain Numbers | Clean, but says nothing about money or tech |
| Decoded Daily | Catchy, generic; could be any niche |
| Follow the Cents | Fun pun, reads as frugality content, which this isn't |

## Visual identity (round 2)

- **Motif:** a gold coin opened like a car hood, gears showing underneath. Money on the outside, mechanism inside. Every asset carries it or its gear fragment.
- **Palette:** near-black `#0B0F14`, amber `#F2A33A` (the glow, the word HOOD), teal `#2FD1C4` (one thin accent line, never a fill), cream `#F3EEE4` (type).
- **Type:** heavy geometric grotesk capitals for titles (Space Grotesk Bold, the same family the app uses), small caps for taglines, one thin teal underline under the title.
- **Rules:** one light source, lots of black, no gradients on type, no stock-chart clichés, the gear never spins in logos.

## Writing

**Channel name:** Money Under the Hood
**Handle:** @moneyunderthehood (fallbacks: @moneyunderhood, @underthehoodmoney)
**Tagline (banner / bio line 1):** How money and tech actually work. One thing a day.

**YouTube description:**
> One money or tech mechanism a day, in about 30 seconds. Compound interest, index funds, credit scores, what a GPU does, what a 1% fee really costs. Every number comes with its source on screen. No tips, no tickers, no "buy this": just how the thing works, so you can decide for yourself.
>
> New short every day. Full week in one video every Sunday.
> Not financial advice. I'm building my own money app in public; the build logs are here too.

**Instagram / TikTok bio (150 characters):**
> How money & tech actually work, 30s a day. Sources on screen. Not advice.

**Standard pinned comment:**
> Source for today's numbers: [link]. Not advice, just the mechanism. What should I explain next?

## Setup checklist (needs your logins)

1. YouTube: create a channel named Money Under the Hood, claim the handle, upload icon and banner, paste the description, add links to Instagram and TikTok. Settings → Channel → Audience: "No, set this channel as not made for kids". Upload defaults: category Education, add "altered or synthetic content" disclosure.
2. Instagram: creator account, same icon, bio above, link to YouTube.
3. TikTok: same icon and bio; enable the "AI-generated content" label on uploads.
4. Higgsfield Earn: connect the YouTube and Instagram accounts, add the verification code to each bio.
5. Post day 1 on all three the same evening; pin the source comment.

## Where else to post

- **YouTube Shorts** is home; it is the only one of the three with real ad revenue for this format once you reach the Partner Program.
- **Instagram Reels and TikTok:** post the same file daily. No payout to count on, but they are where the follows come from early, and TikTok's search is strong for "how does X work".
- **Sunday compilation** (the week's seven shorts cut together, 4 to 5 minutes) on YouTube long-form and TikTok: that is where kids-style watch-time revenue actually lives for an explainer channel, and it qualifies for TikTok's over-one-minute rewards.
- **Build-log shorts** (day 4 each week) also go on X and LinkedIn as native video; that audience follows builders.
- **Reddit** only as answers, never as links: reply in r/personalfinance or r/explainlikeimfive threads with the explanation in text and no promotion; the profile does the marketing.
- Skip paid ads until ten shorts are up and one has clearly outperformed; boost that one.
